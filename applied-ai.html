ABe clear about the tool

AI, automation, or both?

Not every automation project is an AI project, and treating it that way wastes money and adds risk. We choose the simplest tool that fits the work.

01Automation

Use it when the rules are stable and predictable. Deterministic steps, reliable and cheap to run: no model required.

02AI

Use it when the work needs interpretation, summarisation or flexible language understanding that fixed rules can’t capture.

03Both

Combine them when AI must feed a controlled business workflow: the model handles judgement, automation handles the reliable plumbing.

We also keep the categories distinct in delivery: deterministic automation, AI-assisted workflows, generative AI, and local or private AI models each have their place, and each gets human review and governance.

BWhere we fit

You may need us when…

  • Employees repeatedly copy information between documents and systems by hand.
  • Reporting takes days of manual consolidation before anyone can act on it.
  • Teams have access to AI tools but no reliable model for actually implementing them.
  • Management wants AI but hasn’t identified a genuinely useful first use case.
  • Sensitive information makes public AI services unsuitable for the task.
  • A proof of concept exists but was never integrated into operational work.
  • Staff spend too much time searching, summarising, formatting or classifying information.
  • The same question gets answered from scratch every time because past answers live in someone's head, not a searchable system.

EResponsible implementation

AI you can put your name to.

Practical AI has to be defensible as well as useful. We build with these principles in place from the start.

  • Data minimisation: only what the task genuinely needs.
  • Human review at the points that carry risk.
  • Traceability: you can see why an output was produced.
  • Access control over data and models.
  • Realistic performance expectations, tested against real cases.
  • Avoiding unnecessary AI where a simpler tool is safer.
